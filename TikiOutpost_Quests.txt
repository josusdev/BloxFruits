Tiki Outpost Raid Boss

Tyrant of the Skies (Lv. 2600 Raid Boss): 

Summoned by completing the ritual of killing 300 enemies in Tiki Outpost to light 
up the falcon statues' eyes and breaking the surrounding vases. Defeating him is 
required to unlock the Submerged Island entry. 

Level to Finish Island

Finish Level: 2550
